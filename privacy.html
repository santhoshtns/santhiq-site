Privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026. SanthIQ is a private app used by one household on its own phone. It is not offered to the public.

Where data is kept

All of SanthIQ's data is stored on the owner's phone in an encrypted database. Backups are encrypted on the phone with a key derived from the owner's passphrase before they are saved anywhere; whoever stores a backup file sees only encrypted data. No data is sent to the developer, and no server is run for SanthIQ.

Google account data

SanthIQ signs in only to its own Google account, created for this app, and never to anyone's personal or work account. With the permissions the owner grants, it:

  • reads email in that account (bank and card alerts the owner forwards to it) to record transactions;
  • reads the documents the owner copies into that account's Google Drive, without changing them;
  • creates and writes files only in the app's own folder in that account's Drive;
  • reads calendars shared with that account, to show appointments and due dates.

This information is processed on the owner's phone only, to show the owner their own finances and reminders.

SanthIQ's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, not transferred to anyone, not read by any person other than the owner, and not used to develop, improve or train AI or machine learning models.

Removing access and data

The owner can remove SanthIQ's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app deletes its data from the phone; encrypted backup files can be deleted wherever they are kept.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue.
