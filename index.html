SanthIQ

SanthIQ is a private personal-finance and life-admin app for one household. It runs on the owner's own Android phone. It is not offered to the public and is not in any app store.

What it does

  • Keeps a ledger of the household's own bank and card transactions, reconciled to bank statements.
  • Reminds the owner of bills, renewals and document expiry dates.
  • Reads the bank alert emails that the owner forwards to the app's own Google account, the documents the owner copies into that account's Drive (read only), and the calendars shared with it.

Privacy

Everything stays on the owner's phone, encrypted. Read the privacy policy.
